# Management memo — a delivery miss is also a cash decision

**Audience:** Scripted TV FP&A, Production Finance and Business Affairs  
**Status:** Independent fictional case, prepared September 29, 2026  
**Decision:** Rephase the outlook and protect liquidity while challenging permanent cost overruns.

The simulated slate's FY26 revenue falls from $320m to $265m. Last Platform moves from FY26 Q4 to FY27 Q1, shifting $55m of revenue across the fiscal boundary. Signal House moves one quarter within FY26, affecting quarterly reporting but not the full-year delivery count. Opposing fee revisions net to zero at the slate level; their title-level effects still require explanation.

FY26 contribution decreases $17.25m: $8.25m from delivery timing and $9m from higher costs on titles delivered during FY26. Including the delayed title, full-horizon contribution falls from $50m to $39m. That $11m reduction persists after deliveries catch up. A claim that the entire performance shortfall is temporary would therefore be wrong.

Quarterly cash is a different story from contribution. The model shows a maximum cumulative funding need of **$123.58m at the end of FY26 Q3**, assuming no opening cash, financing or other slate cash flows. This is a planning exposure, not an assertion that Sony needs that amount of funding. The six-quarter reconciliation confirms that all modeled receivables are collected and remaining production assets/payables run off by the horizon end.

## Proposed response

| Action | Proposed owner | Evidence needed | Success measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reconfirm acceptance milestones and delivery dates for S03 and S06 | Production / Distribution | Approved delivery schedule and customer acceptance terms | Forecast date vs accepted delivery date |
| Separate permanent EAC changes from timing in the next forecast | Production Finance / FP&A | Latest cost report, approved commitments and contingency | Forecast cost vs final cost by title |
| Prepare a funding view through the collection of the delayed title | Treasury / FP&A | Contract collection dates, receivables ageing, cash spend commitments | Cash forecast error and overdue collections |
| Evaluate earlier customer milestones only where contractually achievable | Business Affairs | Proposed terms and acceptance gates | Reduced cash exposure after all concessions |

Do not accelerate revenue merely to meet budget. First establish the contractual recognition conditions with Accounting. Do not treat the model's evenly spread remaining production spending as a procurement commitment; replace it with approved spend schedules before operational use.

## Interview discussion

The analyst's contribution is to explain **what moved, why it moved, when cash follows, and which portion is recoverable**. In this case, pushing management to distinguish an $8.25m timing effect from $9m of current-year cost deterioration improves the quality of the decision. It does not guarantee savings or predict Sony's actual outcome.
